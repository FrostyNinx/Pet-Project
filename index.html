What's New v1.0.3

Here are the latest features and improvements in Pixel Pet Studio:

✂️
Drag & Highlight Selection Tool
Select pixel clusters with animated marching-ants outline. Drag to move anywhere in frame or carry across timeline frames to duplicate designs. Includes Rotate 90° CW/CCW and Flip H/V tools.
👻
Ghost Active Drawing Layer
Toggle your active drawing layer to 45% transparency so you can see right through your ink to the underlying onion skin ghost layers while animating.
🏁
Grid Printed Over Paint Toggle
Choose whether the pixel grid renders beneath your paint or crisp and bright on top of painted pixels so cell boundaries stay distinct even over solid colors.
🎯
P2P Cursor Isolation by Page & Frame
Partner cursors are strictly scoped to the exact clip and frame index you are editing. No more ghost partner cursors across different tabs or frames!
⚠️
Strict Version Mismatch Protection
P2P handshakes enforce strict version matching, blocking older mismatched clients from connecting to prevent animation data corruption.
⛶
Fullscreen Animation Preview
Enlarged preview box with a 1-click Fullscreen cinema mode and custom FPS playback speed slider to preview pet animations smoothly.
🔊
Studio Audio & Dedicated Settings Tab
Tactile drawing and button sounds with customizable volume sliders, tool mute toggles, and multi-monitor roaming controls housed in the new Settings tab.
